# Chakradhaar Tukda

```text
Terekete taka ta terekete dha ti
Dhage na dha terekete dha ti dha -
Dhage na dha terekete dha ti dha -
Dhage na dha terekete dha ti dha -
Repeat the whole composition thrice
```
