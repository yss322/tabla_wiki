# Tabla Beginner Lessons

A practice companion for the beginner curriculum: four semesters of exercises,
kaaydas, relas, and tukdas. Every composition is written out in **bols** — the
spoken syllables of the drum — with a demonstration video wherever one has been
recorded. Recite along, then play.

## How to practise

Recite the bols aloud with a hand-clap before you play them. Keep *tere kete*
inside the black **gaab** at the centre of the drum, and let the left-hand
fingers rest on the edge. Take one palta at a time.

1. Relax the hand
2. Correct the shoulder posture
3. Recite with a hand-clap
4. Left-hand fingers touch the edge
5. *Tere kete* stays within the black gaab
6. Beginners play straight *tete* only — no reverse *tete*
7. No more than three paltas in an hour

## For teachers

- Check the student has a tabla set and has registered for the term.
- Ask students to bring a composition notebook for their own notation.
- Keep classes to a maximum of eight students.
- Assess each student's level individually, practise as a group, then have
  every student play alone in class.
