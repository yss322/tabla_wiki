# Dha te te Dha te te Kaayda

*For beginners, teach full hand tete (3 fingers)*

## Theme

```text
Dha te te Dha te te Dha Dha
te te Dha ge Tina Kena
Ta tete Ta tete Dha Dha
te te Dha ge Dhina Gena
```

## Tihaai

```text
Dha te te Dha te te Dha Dha
te te Dha ge Dhina Gena
Dha - Dha Dha te te Dhage
Dhina Gena Dha - Dha Dha te te
Dhage Dhina Gena Dha - -
play thrice
```
